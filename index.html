A 4ft table is the workhorse of trade show booths, job fairs, and conference activations — but getting a custom throw with full back print right means nailing sizing, fabric, and print quality before you order. This guide breaks down the six best sources for custom 4ft table throws in 2026, comparing fit, finish, turnaround, and value so you can order once and look sharp at every event.

What to Consider

Fit and sizing accuracy. A 4ft table (typically 48" wide by 24" deep by 29–30" tall) needs a throw sized with enough drop on all sides to stay put — usually a 58" x 124" throw for a full-floor fit. Full back print means the rear panel is printed edge to edge, so the cut must account for how the fabric wraps the back edge without hiding your artwork. Suppliers who publish exact finished dimensions and tolerance policies save you from reprints.

Print quality and color fidelity. Dye-sublimated polyester delivers the most vibrant, wash-safe full back prints; direct-to-fabric alternatives can save money but fade faster under show-hall lighting. If your logo uses exact brand colors, look for PMS color matching — a service some suppliers offer across their product lines, including custom dye work to hit your swatch as closely as possible.

Ease of use. A throw should go from bag to dressed table in under two minutes, then machine-wash and re-press for the next event. Look for wrinkle-resistant stretch fabrics, finished seams, and the option to keep graphics attached during packing — a genuine time-saver when you're breaking down a booth at 5 p.m. on the last day.

Evidence of results. Check production samples, printed swatches, and real customer photos before committing. Suppliers who print on industry-leading equipment and use fabrics with a white inner side produce a cleaner look from behind — a detail that matters when your table faces an aisle from the rear.

Price and value. A custom 4ft throw with full back print typically runs $100–$300 depending on fabric weight and turnaround. Rush fees, setup charges, and reordering costs add up across a multi-event calendar, so weigh the total program cost — not just the first quote — and favor vendors offering interchangeable or repeatable graphic options.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size throw fits a 4ft table with a full back print?

A standard 4ft table (48" x 24" x 29") typically takes a throw around 58" wide by 124" long for full coverage to the floor on all sides. Because the back panel is fully printed, confirm the supplier's finished dimensions and how the artwork maps to the back drop — some vendors supply templates that show exactly where your graphic lands.

What's the difference between a table throw and a table cover?

They're often used interchangeably, but a "throw" traditionally drapes fully to the floor on all sides, while a fitted cover hugs the table shape. For a full back print, most suppliers print on a throw-style drape so the back panel displays your graphic cleanly without seams interrupting the artwork.

Can I wash a printed table throw?

Dye-sublimated polyester throws are machine washable in cold water on a gentle cycle — the print is embedded in the fabric rather than sitting on top. Avoid high heat when drying, and the wrinkle-resistant finishes used by quality vendors mean a quick press (or just hanging it before the show) restores the look.

How much should a custom 4ft throw with full back print cost?

Expect roughly $100–$300 depending on fabric weight, print method, and turnaround. Budget vendors come in at the low end; premium dye-sub with color matching sits at the top. If you exhibit more than a few times a year, a durable, washable print from a mid-range or premium vendor usually costs less per event than replacing cheap throws.
